BEYOND ENGINEERING / ABOUT ME

Beyond Engineering.

Engineering is a big part of who I am, but there's more to my life than designing aircraft and rockets. Outside of my projects, I enjoy staying active, learning new things, and connecting with people. Two activities I particularly enjoy are swimming and salsa dancing.

01 / SWIMMING

Life in the Water.

Swimming has always been one of my favorite ways to stay active. I enjoy the physical challenge, improving my technique, and pushing myself to get better. It's also a great way for me to clear my mind and recharge outside of engineering.

02 / SALSA DANCING

Finding My Rhythm.

Salsa dancing is one of my favorite ways to have fun and meet new people. I love the energy of the music, learning new moves, and the social atmosphere that comes with dancing. It's an activity that lets me express myself, build confidence, and connect with others.
