01 / ABOUT ME

Aerospace Engineer in the Making. Builder.
Rocketry Enthusiast.

University of Central Florida
B.S. Aerospace Engineering · Expected Spring 2027

I’m an Aerospace Engineering student at the University of Central Florida who enjoys hands-on engineering, from designing and manufacturing components to testing systems and learning from the results.

Through Knights Experimental Rocketry (KXR), the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA), and other engineering projects, I’ve gained experience with high-powered rockets, RC aircraft, aerodynamic test vehicles, and recovery systems, developing skills in CAD, CFD, simulation, fabrication, flight testing, controls, and systems integration.

I'm particularly interested in propulsion, aircraft and rocket design, and aerospace vehicle development, and I hope to keep turning ideas into real, tested, and reliable systems.

01Rocket Recovery

Aerodynamically Controlled Rocket Recovery System

Developing an autonomous recovery system to guide a 4-8 lb rocket from an estimated 2,000 ft apogee to a landing zone within a 100 ft radius. The concept combines a drogue parachute, steerable parafoil, wind aware MATLAB/Simulink guidance, OpenRocket trajectory analysis, and custom structures.
